  • Fantasy Terrain Brushes: Stamp realistic mountain ranges, ancient forests, desert dunes, swamps, and hills with a single stroke.
  • Multi-Layer Architecture: Create independent layers, adjust opacity, reorder, and bake layers directly into the base canvas.
